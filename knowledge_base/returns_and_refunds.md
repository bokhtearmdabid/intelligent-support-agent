# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
You can request a return within 7 days of the delivery date. The 7 days are counted from the day the order is marked as delivered. After 7 days we cannot accept return requests, except for items covered by the warranty.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
If your item arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, report it within 7 days of delivery. Please keep the original packaging and share clear photos of the item and the box. An unboxing video is helpful but not required. We will offer a replacement if the item is in stock. If a replacement is not available, we will offer a refund.

## Change of mind
We accept change-of-mind returns only for unopened items in sealed original packaging, within 3 days of delivery. Opened electronics cannot be returned for change of mind. The customer pays the return shipping cost for change-of-mind returns. We cover the return shipping for damaged, defective or wrong items.

## How refunds are approved
Every refund and replacement request is reviewed by our support team. The request is not complete until a support agent approves it. You will be informed of the decision.

## Refund timeline
After approval, refunds are sent to the original payment method. bKash and Nagad refunds take 3 to 5 working days. Card refunds take 7 to 10 working days. For Cash on Delivery orders, we refund to your bKash or Nagad number, or to your bank account, after you provide the details.

## Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, digital products, and items damaged by misuse, water or unauthorised repair cannot be returned or refunded.
