# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery time
Orders are processed within 1 working day. Delivery time after dispatch: inside Dhaka 1 to 2 working days, other major cities 2 to 3 working days, and the rest of Bangladesh 3 to 5 working days. Delays can happen during public holidays, bad weather or hartals.

## Delivery charge
Delivery inside Dhaka costs 60 BDT. Delivery outside Dhaka costs 120 BDT. Orders above 5,000 BDT get free delivery anywhere in Bangladesh.

## Couriers and tracking
We deliver through Pathao and Steadfast. When your order is shipped, you receive the courier name and a tracking number by SMS and email. You can also see them under My Orders in your account.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address only before the order is shipped. Once an order is shipped, the address cannot be changed. Please contact support as soon as possible if the address is wrong.

## Failed delivery
The courier tries to deliver 2 times. If both attempts fail because the customer was unreachable, the parcel is returned to us and the order may be cancelled.
