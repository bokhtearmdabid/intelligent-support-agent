# Payments and Cancellation Policy

## Payment methods
We accept bKash, Nagad, debit and credit cards, and Cash on Delivery. Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to 15,000 BDT.

## Payment failed but money deducted
If your payment fails but the money was deducted, the amount is usually reversed automatically within 3 to 5 working days. If it is not reversed after 5 working days, contact support with your transaction ID.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order for free before it is shipped. Cancellation requests are reviewed by the support team. After the order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery or request a return under the Returns and Refunds Policy.

## Payment security
ShopEase staff and the support assistant will never ask for your password, OTP, PIN or full card number. Never share these with anyone, even if they claim to be from ShopEase or bKash.
